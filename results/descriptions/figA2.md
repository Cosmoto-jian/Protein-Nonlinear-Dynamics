# figA2

Fig. A.2 gauge diagnostics for 8 structures. Six isostatic anchors carry reactions of 2.4e-03 to 0.38 of the load and distort the solution by up to 1.07 A; the reference-equilibrated load increases the distortion by more than 0.01 A in 5 structures. The anchored tangent is 11 to 42 times softer than with the Eckart conditions.
