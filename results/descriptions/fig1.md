# fig1

Fig. 1 model and load: C-alpha truss of MscL (2OAR, r_c = 10 A) with 625 nodes and 5563 elements; 265 loaded outer-shell nodes inside the membrane slab (329 membrane nodes). One 4 A layer polygon with 21 vertices and the nodal forces tau dA/dx, which act only on the outer shell. Panel a overlays the C-alpha network on the molecular surface (transparency 0.95); the 10 elements of one loaded node are drawn dark. Right: formulation and line of the paper.
